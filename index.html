How this is calculated

Investment value = weighted upgrade costs + optional production lost during construction. Payback runtime = investment value ÷ weighted marginal output per hour. Profit starts after construction and becomes positive at the displayed total break-even time. Multi-level routes assume back-to-back upgrades with no production between them. Base prerequisite costs are excluded because they benefit the whole account, not only this producer.

Costs and production are static Android-client values. Times apply the selected additive Building Speed and free-finish threshold; results cannot predict server overrides or alliance-help timing. Configuration files stay on your device—the planner does not upload or store account state.
